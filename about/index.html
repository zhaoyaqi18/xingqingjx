Our Story

Built on Experience, Driven by Quality

Founded in Gongyi, Zhengzhou — the heart of China's heavy machinery industry — XingQing Machinery has grown from a specialized workshop into a comprehensive mining equipment manufacturer with two dedicated divisions: Machinery Manufacturing and Casting.

Over the past 16 years, we have established long-term partnerships with research institutes, continuously improving our products and developing new technologies to meet the evolving needs of the global mining industry. Our equipment is now operating in more than 50 countries across Africa, Southeast Asia, South America, and the Middle East.

We believe in providing not just machines, but complete solutions — from process design and equipment selection to installation, commissioning, and ongoing technical support. Every machine we build reflects our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction.
