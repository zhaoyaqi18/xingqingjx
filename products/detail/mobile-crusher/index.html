Crawler vs. Tire TypeCrawler vs Tire

Mobile crushing stations are divided into crawler and tire types by chassis travel method, each with advantages for different conditions and budgets. XingQing Machinery offers both chassis types with jaw, impact, hammer, cone crusher options for primary/medium/fine crushing full process.

Crawler Crawler Type

  • Travel mode: Hydraulic self-driven, remote control, no tractor needed
  • Terrain adaptability: Strong traction, low ground pressure, can climb (max 30°), adapts to mountains, pits, muddy terrain
  • Travel speed: Crawler self-propelled 1-3 km/h, convenient short-distance; long-distance needs trailer transport
  • Power options: Diesel drive / hybrid / external power optional, operable without grid coverage
  • Investment cost: Higher unit price, hydraulic system and crawler undercarriage costly
  • Typical scenarios: Mining, tunnel muck, large construction waste processing, mountain road projects

Tire Tire / Wheel Type

  • Travel mode: Semi-trailer towed, needs tractor head on road
  • Terrain: Suitable for paved roads and flat sites; high chassis, small turning radius for flexible plant movement
  • Travel speed: Road towing 60-80 km/h, suitable for medium-long distance, fast setup on arrival
  • Power: Mainly external power, diesel generator as backup
  • Investment: Lower unit price, chassis and travel system cost ~30-50% less than crawler type
  • Typical scenarios: Urban construction waste recycling, road construction, small-medium aggregate plants, frequent relocation within site

Selection AdviceSelection Advice

  1. Choose chassis by terrain & relocation needs: Rough terrain, slopes, no grid — choose crawler (self-driven, remote control). Flat site, paved roads, medium-long relocation, limited budget — choose tire (towed, 30-50% lower cost). Both can use same crusher types, core difference is only the travel system.
  2. Choose crusher by material & product: Feed 300-800mm large, hard ore (granite, basalt) → crawler jaw station; construction waste, medium-hard (limestone, waste concrete) → crawler/tire impact station, good shape; wet soft material (coal gangue, shale) → hammer mobile station; hard rock fine crushing (iron, copper ore) → cone mobile station.
  3. Choose model by capacity: Individual/small yard (5-30 t/h): tire small mobile crusher; medium project (50-150 t/h): tire impact/jaw mobile station;ion; large mine or construction waste line (150-400 t/h) → crawler jaw/impact mobile station, can pair with mobile screen for flexible line.
  4. Accessory equipment: When a single mobile crusher produces mixed material, add a mobile screen station (same tire/crawler chassis) in series for mobile crushing+screening line. For high dust, confirm spray and enclosed cover; diesel models: pay attention to fuel consumption and maintenance intervals.
