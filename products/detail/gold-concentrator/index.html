Centrifugal Gold Concentrator

Product OverviewProduct Overview

Centrifugal Gold Concentrator (also known as water jacket centrifuge, gold water jacket centrifuge) is one of the modern gravity separation equipment. Developed and improved based on traditional centrifuges, it enhances gravity separation through centrifugal force field, allowing heavy mineral particles to fully settle. The equipment features large capacity, wide particle size range, simple operation and maintenance. Widely used as rougher or cleaner for gold, tin, tungsten, titanium, hematite and other metallic minerals. Electromagnetic speed-regulating motor enables stepless stroke adjustment, reducing water consumption by 30%-40%, floor space by 1/3, with recovery rate reaching 50-60% or more.

Working PrincipleWorking Principle

1

Pulp Feeding

Gold-bearing minerals enter the bottom of the dual-layer centrifugal drum through the upper feed pipe in pulp form. Pulp concentration controlled below 50%, feed particle size ≤6mm.

2

Centrifugal Stratification

High-speed centrifugal force (hundreds of G) causes the pulp to slide upward along the inner cone. Heavy minerals (gold, tin, etc.) settle between the annular riffles, while light minerals continue moving upward.

3

Back-flush Fluidization

Pressurized water enters the water jacket through the hollow shaft, sprays through inner-layer orifices into the riffle gaps to form back-flush water, fluidizing the material layer between riffles. Heavy minerals deposit to the bottom while light minerals are displaced and discharged.

4

Concentrate Collection

Light minerals continue moving upward to the upper opening and are discharged through the tailings chute. After a period of operation, the machine stops, and the gold-bearing heavy minerals deposited between the riffles are cleaned out, completing one beneficiation cycle.

Dual-layer Centrifugal Drum Structure
Pulp → Feed pipe into drum bottom → Sliding upward in centrifugal field → Heavy particles settle between riffles → Back-flush fluidization stratification → Light particles displaced to tailings chute → Stop for concentrate collection

Application FieldsApplication Fields

Placer Gold Recovery

Replaces traditional sluices and mercury plate operations for placer gold roughing and cleaning. DH-20/30 suitable for small placer gold operations, DH-60/80 for medium to large placer gold mines.

Vein Gold Gravity Separation

Installed in grinding-classification circuit to recover liberated coarse monomer gold early, reducing over-grinding and cyanide consumption. Also used for scavenging recovery of flotation tailings.

Multi-Metal Heavy Sand Cleaning

Separation of heavy minerals (tin, tungsten, lead-zinc, etc.) from light minerals. Centrifugal field enhances density differences, achieving good recovery even for fine (-0.074mm) heavy minerals.

Tailings Reprocessing & Environmental Recovery

Selection AdviceSelection Advice

  1. Select by throughput: 0.25-0.3 T/h choose DH-20 (lab/small operation), 2-3 T/h choose DH-30 (small mine), 10-15 T/h choose DH-60 (medium mine), 35-40 T/h choose DH-80 (large mine).
  2. Placer vs Vein Gold: Placer gold ore has already liberated gold particles, can be directly fed into centrifuge for cleaning; vein gold requires crushing and grinding to ≤6mm, optimal monomer gold recovery when grinding fineness -200 mesh reaches 65%+.
  3. Process position: Can be used as rougher/cleaner for placer gold; for vein gold, recommended to install at ball mill discharge or after classifier overflow to recover coarse gold early and prevent over-grinding.
  4. Water supply requirements: Back-flush water must be clean (no large sediment), water pressure matched to model (0.05-0.18 MPa). Circulating water system available for water-scarce areas, low water consumption (30-40% less than traditional equipment).
