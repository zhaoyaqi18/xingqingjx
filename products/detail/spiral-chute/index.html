Spiral Chute

Product OverviewProduct Overview

The Spiral Chute is a gravity mineral processing equipment that utilizes differences in density, particle size and shape of mineral particles, forming a thin-layer flow in an inclined spiral trough with the help of water flow and gravity to achieve mineral separation. First successfully developed in China in 1977, it combines the technical features of spiral concentrators, shaking tables and centrifugal concentrators. The main body is assembled from fiberglass spiral segments with polyurethane wear-resistant coating on the inner surface, equipped with a splitter, feed trough and product cutters. Pulp flows by gravity without external power, and no wash water is added during separation. Excellent separation effect for minerals with significant density differences such as tantalum, niobium, tin, tungsten, gold, titanium, iron. The DI Series covers six outer diameters from φ400-2000mm, 1-4 starts, with production capacity of 0.5-45 m³/h.

Working PrincipleWorking Principle

1

Cross-section circulation formation

Pulp enters the spiral trough from the top feeder. As it flows along the spiral surface, it continuously changes direction, generating centrifugal force. This centrifugal force creates a transverse liquid level gradient from the outer edge to the inner edge in the cross-section, generating secondary cross-section circulation.

2

Heavy-light mineral stratification

High-density heavy mineral particles settle to the trough bottom under the combined action of gravity, centrifugal force and water shear force, moving slowly along the inner side of the spiral trough. Low-density light minerals suspend in the upper water layer, drifting outward or being carried away by the water flow.

3

End cutter separation

Adjustable cutters are installed at the bottom end of the spiral trough to direct the stratified pulp flow into concentrate, middlings and tailings buckets respectively. No wash water, no moving parts, no external power throughout the process, achieving precise three-stage separation.

Cross-section Circulation Separation Principle
Pulp fed to top → Spiral trough generates centrifugal field → Secondary cross-section circulation → Heavy particles settle inward → Light particles suspend outward → End cutter → Concentrate / Middlings / Tailings

Product FeaturesProduct Features

Zero energy consumption, eco-friendly

Relies entirely on gravity pulp flow for separation: no moving parts, no motor, no wash water. No chemical reagents added. No secondary pollution from wastewater, exhaust gas or solid waste. One of the most energy-efficient and environmentally friendly mineral processing equipment.

High fine-particle separation accuracy

Excellent separation performance for 0.02-0.3mm fine and ultra-fine minerals. Secondary cross-section circulation enables precise separation of heavy and light minerals in thin-layer flow, with enrichment ratio and recovery significantly better than traditional chutes.

Extremely low operating and maintenance cost

No moving parts means zero wear part replacement, zero lubrication, no breakdown risk. Only periodic inspection of spiral trough lining wear required. Nearly zero maintenance cost in daily operation.

High capacity, small footprint

Multi-layer multi-start configuration (1-4 starts) greatly increases capacity within limited space. DI2000 with 4-start configuration achieves 45 m³/h throughput with only 5.7 m² footprint, extremely high processing efficiency per unit area.

Simple operation, strong adaptability

No professional operator required. Once adjusted, runs stably long-term. Strong adaptability to fluctuations in feed rate, concentration, particle size and grade. Stable separation within pulp concentration range of 20%-50%.

Corrosion-proof, long service life

FRP material + PU wear-resistant lining: lightweight, moisture-proof, rust-proof, corrosion-resistant. Stable performance in both acidic and alkaline pulp environments. Design life far exceeds metal chutes.

Application FieldsApplication Fields

Iron ore fine gravity separation

Processes fine hematite and specularite after magnetic separation or flotation, with recovery much higher than traditional chutes. DI1200-2000 multi-start configuration suitable for roughing and scavenging in medium-large iron ore processing plants.

Tungsten, tin, tantalum, niobium cleaning

These high-density minerals have large density differences from gangue, and the spiral chute's cross-section circulation provides excellent separation. DI900-1200 suitable for tungsten-tin cleaning, DI600 for laboratory or small-scale Ta-Nb testing.

Placer/rock gold gravity separation

Handles roughing enrichment of placer gold and gravity recovery in rock gold grinding circuits, extracting liberated coarse gold early. Multi-stage series connection can progressively upgrade concentrate grade.

Beach placer and ilmenite

Processing heavy minerals such as ilmenite, zircon and monazite from beach placer deposits. FRP material is seawater corrosion resistant, very suitable for coastal and beach placer roughing enrichment.

Selection AdviceSelection Advice

  1. Select Outer Dia. by Capacity:Small test/lab: DI400-600 (0.2-1.2 m³/h); small mine: DI900 (2-3 m³/h); medium mine: DI1200 (4-6 m³/h); large mine: DI1500-2000 (10-45 m³/h).
  2. Select Pitch by Particle Size Range:Coarse minerals (0.1-4mm): pitch ratio 0.6 (e.g., DI2000 pitch 1200mm); fine minerals (0.02-0.2mm): optional pitch ratio 0.4 (e.g., DI900 pitch 360mm). Smaller pitch gives higher separation accuracy but lower capacity.
  3. Starts Configuration:Single start: high-precision cleaning; multiple starts: capacity increases proportionally but accuracy slightly decreases. Rougher operation: prefer 3-4 starts for fast processing of large pulp volumes; cleaning operation: 1-2 starts for guaranteed grade.
  4. Multi-Stage Series Process:Single spiral chute has limited enrichment ratio. Recommend rougher-cleaner-scavenger multi-stage series: rougher stage with DI1500 multi-start for fast tailings rejection, cleaner stage with DI900 fewer starts for grade improvement, achieving both recovery and grade improvement.
