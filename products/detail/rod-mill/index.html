MBS Rod Mill

Product OverviewProduct Overview

The rod mill is mainly used for coarse grinding of materials above 1mm. When grinding coarse materials, the rod mill has higher throughput than a ball mill of the same specification. Product particle size generally does not exceed 4.7mm maximum, minimum not less than 0.4mm, with feed size 80% less than 20mm to 4mm, and max feed size up to 50mm. The rod mill sand maker is versatile and a practical and reliable sand shaping equipment. Suitable for fine and medium crushing of various hard and brittle materials including abrasives, refractories, cement, quartz sand, steel shot, slag powder, copper ore, iron ore, gold ore, concrete aggregate, and asphalt materials. Especially suitable for adjusting fineness modulus in manufactured sand production — an efficient and energy-saving stone crushing and sand making equipment.

Working PrincipleWorking Principle

1

Feed

Material (particle size ≤50mm) is continuously fed into the rotating cylinder through the feed opening. A certain number of steel rods are pre-loaded into the cylinder as grinding media.

2

Rod Lift

The cylinder rotates at 17-29 r/min. Steel rods are carried up to a certain height by the liner under centrifugal force and friction, in a cascading or cataracting state.

3

Line Contact Grinding

When steel rods fall, they strike material through line contact — first hitting coarse particles then grinding fine particles, reducing over-grinding. Rolling and grinding between steel rods further mills the material.

4

Discharge

Ground material is discharged by overflow and the thrust of continuous feeding, entering the next process stage. The rod mill discharge end is equipped with a discharge grate plate to prevent steel rods from discharging.

Power Transmission Path
Motor→Reducer→Pinion→Ring Gear→Cylinder→Liner→Steel Rod→Material

Product FeaturesProduct Features

Line Contact Grinding, Uniform Particle Size

Steel rods use line contact instead of ball mill's point contact. During grinding, they first strike coarse particles then crush fines, resulting in more uniform discharge particle size with good continuous gradation.

Low Over-grinding

Steel rods preferentially act on coarse particles when striking ore. Already crushed fine particles are not easily re-crushed, effectively avoiding over-grinding with low powder content in finished product.

High Coarse Grinding Capacity

When grinding coarse materials above 1mm, the rod mill has higher throughput than a ball mill of the same specification, suitable for coarse grinding stage.

Energy Saving

Saves over 40% power compared to traditional sand making equipment with low operating costs. Advanced controllable feed/discharge technology with appropriate grinding media increases output by nearly 50%.

Excellent Sand Shaping Effect

High medium sand ratio, good particle shape with few sharp edges. Especially suitable for adjusting fineness modulus in manufactured sand production and sand shaping.

Wide Application Range

Can process various hard and brittle materials including quartzite, iron ore, copper ore, gold ore, slag and pebbles. Both dry and wet types available, adapting to different process requirements.

Reliable Operation, Convenient Maintenance

Mature structural design with reliable flange connection between cylinder and end covers. Steel rod replacement is simple with low daily maintenance, suitable for continuous production.

Application FieldsApplication Fields

Manufactured Sand

Core equipment in manufactured sand production lines, especially suitable for sand shaping and fineness modulus adjustment. Pebbles, granite, limestone etc. produce well-shaped manufactured sand after rod milling.

Metal Mines

Coarse grinding stage for iron ore, copper ore, gold ore and lead-zinc ore, providing qualified particle size for subsequent ball milling and flotation. Classic choice for single-stage open-circuit grinding.

Building Materials & Refractories

Cement raw material pre-grinding, quartz sand fine grinding, refractory raw material processing. Grinding and reuse of industrial byproducts such as steel shot and slag powder.

Chemical & Glass

Grinding operations with high uniformity requirements for chemical and glass raw materials. Processing, crushing and sand making of ore materials such as potassium feldspar and quartzite.

Selection AdviceSelection Advice

  1. Capacity Matching:Small sand plants or labs choose MBS0918~1224 (3-35 t/h); medium sand lines choose MBS1530~2136 (7-135 t/h); large mine beneficiation choose MBS2430~3245 (50-260 t/h).
  2. Feed Size:Max rod mill feed up to 50mm, but ≤25mm recommended for optimal efficiency. Oversize feed should be pre-crushed by a jaw crusher.
  3. Dry vs Wet:Wet rod mill has higher output and less dust, suitable for closed circuit with spiral classifier; dry rod mill suits water-deficient areas and dry production lines.
  4. Steel Rod Gradation:Steel rod diameter is typically Φ40-100mm, length 25-50mm shorter than cylinder. Coarse grinding uses large diameter rods, fine grinding uses small diameter rods. Rod filling rate is 35-45% of cylinder volume.
