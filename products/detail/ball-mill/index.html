Ball Mill

Product OverviewProduct Overview

The Ball Mill is key equipment for grinding crushed materials. It is widely used in cement, silicate products, new building materials, refractories, fertilizers, black and non-ferrous metal beneficiation, and glass ceramics industries, for dry or wet grinding of various ores and other grindable materials. The Ball Mill is suitable for grinding various ores and other materials, widely used in beneficiation, building materials, chemical and other industries, available in dry and wet grinding modes. By discharge method, it can be divided into grid type and overflow type. A certain number of steel balls are loaded into the cylinder as grinding media. When the cylinder rotates, the grinding media are carried up by centrifugal force and friction, then fall to crush the material through the dual action of impact and grinding.

Working PrincipleWorking Principle

1

Feed

Material is continuously fed into the rotating cylinder through the feed device. The cylinder is pre-loaded with a certain quantity and gradation of steel balls as grinding media.

2

Lifting

The cylinder rotates at 60%-80% of critical speed. Steel balls are carried upward by centrifugal force and liner friction.

3

Falling Impact

When the steel balls reach a certain height, gravity overcomes centrifugal force and they fall in a parabolic trajectory, producing intense impact and grinding on the material to achieve crushing and pulverization.

4

Discharge

The ground material flows toward the discharge end driven by material level difference and airflow, discharged through the discharge device. Coarse particles are intercepted by the partition plate and returned for further grinding.

Power Transmission Path
Motor → Reducer → Pinion → Ring Gear → Cylinder → Liner → Grinding Media → Material

Product FeaturesProduct Features

High Capacity

Single unit daily output can reach hundreds of tons, suitable for large-scale industrial continuous production. MQG/MQY/MQS three series cover all scenarios from laboratory to heavy mining.

Dry & Wet Dual Use

Flexible choice of dry or wet grinding based on process requirements. Dry type suitable for cement, coal powder; wet type suitable for beneficiation. Extremely adaptable.

Controllable Discharge Size

By adjusting steel ball gradation (large balls for impact + small balls for grinding), filling rate, and speed, precisely control discharge fineness to meet different downstream process requirements.

Mature & Stable Technology

Classic grinding equipment proven by a century of industrial verification. Stable and reliable operation, can be connected to DCS system for automated control and remote monitoring.

Strong Adaptability

Suitable for various ores and materials with Mohs hardness 1-9, from soft limestone to hard iron ore, all processed efficiently.

Rugged & Durable Structure

Heavy-duty welded cylinder + alloy steel main bearing + high-chrome cast steel liner. Key components designed for long service life under continuous heavy-load conditions.

Easy Maintenance

Modular design, quick replacement of liners and steel balls. Standardized lubrication system, low maintenance cost, short downtime, strong spare parts commonality.

Application FieldsApplication Fields

Mining Industry

Core grinding equipment in metal ore (iron, copper, gold, manganese, etc.) beneficiation plants, achieving full mineral liberation from gangue for subsequent flotation or magnetic separation.

Building Materials Industry

Raw meal grinding (limestone, clay) and cement grinding (clinker) in cement production lines, as well as grinding of gypsum and slag powder.

Chemical Industry

Ultra-fine grinding of chemical raw materials such as fertilizer materials (phosphate rock), pigments (titanium dioxide), and catalyst carriers. With classification equipment, can produce micron-level powders.

Power & Environmental Protection

Coal powder preparation in thermal power plants (steel ball coal mill), grinding coal into fine powder to improve combustion efficiency. Industrial waste slag recycling grinding for resource reuse.

Selection AdviceSelection Advice

  1. Material Hardness: For Mohs hardness 6+ (iron ore, granite), recommend Φ2100 and above with high-chrome cast steel liners and larger diameter steel balls. For soft materials (limestone, coal), medium-small specs are optional.
  2. Capacity Requirements: For daily throughput under 200 tons, recommend Φ1500×3000 ~ Φ1830×4500. For thousand-ton daily output, recommend Φ2400×7000 and above, achieving stable single-unit output of 50-100 t/h.
  3. MQG vs MQY vs MQS: MQG grid type with forced discharge, suitable for coarse grinding and primary grinding. MQY overflow type with gravity discharge by material level difference, suitable for fine grinding. MQS wet type suitable for beneficiation processes, forming a closed circuit with classifier.
  4. Fineness & Cylinder Length: For coarse grinding (discharge 0.3-0.15mm), choose short cylinder (L/D≈1-1.5). For fine grinding (discharge <0.074mm), choose long cylinder (L/D>3), can beCoordination with classification equipment for ultra-fine grinding.
