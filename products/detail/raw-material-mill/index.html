Raw Material Mill

Product OverviewProduct Overview

Raw Material Mill, also known as raw meal mill, is an important grinding equipment in cement production, widely used in building materials, metallurgy, power and chemical industries for grinding (drying and grinding combined) cement raw materials and other materials. Raw meal enters the tube mill after crushing and is ground into fine powder through the movement andextrusion of steel balls. This series saves 35~50% investment compared to traditional equipment at the same scale, meeting raw material supply for 250~2500 t/d cement lines. Drive types: edge drive and center drive, flexibly selectable based on site conditions and capacity scale.

Working PrincipleWorking Principle

1

Feed

Crushed raw material (size <25mm) is continuously fed into the mill cylinder through the feeding device. The cylinder is pre-loaded with steel grinding balls at a certain ratio.

2

Lifting

The cylinder rotates at set speed (15~22 r/min). Steel balls are carried upward by the cylinder wall under centrifugal force and liner friction.

3

Impact & Grinding

Steel balls fall under gravity after reaching a certain height, causing impact crushing of material. Meanwhile, sliding and rolling between balls produce grinding action, gradually fine-grinding the material.

4

Discharge

Fine-ground material is discharged through the discharge device. Hot air can be simultaneously introduced for combined drying and grinding, with finished fineness of 80μm sieve residue 12~15%.

Power Transmission Path
Motor → Main reducer → Pinion → Girth gear → Cylinder → Liner → Grinding media → Material

Product FeaturesProduct Features

High Grinding Efficiency

Single-compartment structure with modular liner design uses differentiated liner configurations for different stages inside the mill, significantly improving grinding efficiency.

High Output

Large end cover and discharge system feature streamlined design, reducing ventilation resistance, increasing effective volume, enabling smooth discharge and greatly improving mill output.

Economical Investment

35~50% lower investment than traditional equipment at the same scale, suitable for cement plants with limited capital to start production quickly, with short payback period.

Combined Drying and Grinding

Hot air can be simultaneously introduced for drying. Raw materials with high moisture can be fed directly into the mill without pre-drying, simplifying the process flow.

Flexible Drive Configuration

Small to medium specs use edge drive (simple structure, easy maintenance); large specs use center drive (smooth transmission, low noise).

Strong Adaptability

Suitable for various cement raw materials including limestone, clay, iron powder, bauxite, etc. Feed size up to 25mm with controllable finished fineness.

Reliable Operation

Heavy-duty welded cylinder + alloy steel bearings. Key components designed for long service life, suitable for 24-hour continuous operation in cement production lines.

Application FieldsApplication Fields

Cement Industry

Core raw meal preparation equipment in cement production lines. Mixes and grinds limestone, clay, iron powder and other raw materials to specified fineness before feeding to the rotary kiln for calcination.

Building Materials Industry

Grinding and processing of raw materials for silicate products, new building materials, refractory materials, etc. Uniform finished particle size meets downstream production requirements.

Power Industry

Preparation of desulfurization limestone powder for thermal power plants. Limestone is ground to below 80μm for flue gas desulfurization, improving desulfurization efficiency.

Metallurgy and Chemical

Grinding of industrial raw materials including bauxite, active lime, aluminum hydroxide, slag, etc. Combined with classification equipment for precise fineness control.

Selection AdviceSelection Advice

  1. Capacity matching: For 250 t/d cement line, recommend Φ2.2×6.5; for 1000 t/d, recommend Φ3×11~Φ3.2×13; for 2500 t/d, recommend Φ3.8×13 and above.
  2. Drive type selection: For specs below Φ3.2m, recommend edge drive (low investment, easy maintenance); for Φ3.5m and above, recommend center drive (smooth transmission, low noise, suitable for continuous heavy load).
  3. Drying requirements: When comprehensive raw material moisture >5%, equip hot air furnace using kiln tail exhaust waste heat to achieve combined drying and grinding, reducing system energy consumption.
  4. Open vs closed circuit: Closed circuit system with classifier allows flexible fineness adjustment and reduces over-grinding, suitable for high fineness raw meal preparation. Open circuit system has simple flow, suitable for small to medium scale.
