Dry Magnetic Roller

Product OverviewProduct Overview

The Dry Magnetic Roller is a permanent magnetic roller-type dry magnetic separator, typically installed as the head pulley (drive pulley) of a belt conveyor. Using the strong magnetic field generated by built-in high-performance permanent magnets, when material is conveyed by belt to the roller and discharged, magnetic material is adsorbed on the belt surface and continues with the roller into the concentrate hopper, while non-magnetic material is discharged normally at the roller end. 5 models cover roller diameters of φ320-630mm, belt widthsCompatible B500/B650, capacity 20-80 t/h, processing size 10-50mm, suitable for dry pre-selection waste removal of lump magnetite and iron removal from non-magnetic materials.

Working PrincipleWorking Principle

1

Material Spreading & Conveying

Material (ore/grain/building materials) is evenly spread on the belt by the belt conveyor and conveyed forward. The dry magnetic roller is installed at the head of the belt conveyor, replacing the ordinary head pulley, driving the belt operation.

2

Magnetic Adsorption & Separation

When material reaches the magnetic roller head, it begins to be discharged. Magnetic ore particles are attracted by the strong magnetic field force from the permanent magnet system inside the roller, adsorbed on the belt surface, and continue moving downward with the roller rotation rather than being thrown off by inertia.

3

Two-Way Ore Collection

Magnetic material is carried by the belt to the bottom of the roller where the magnetic field weakens, naturally falling off into the concentrate hopper. Non-magnetic material is normally thrown off at the roller top into the tailings hopper, completing dry magnetic separation — no washing water, no additional power (using the existing belt conveyor drive).

Belt Head Permanent Magnetic Roller Dry Separation Principle
Material conveyed by belt → Reaches magnetic roller head → Magnetic material adsorbed on belt → Follows roller to bottom
Non-magnetic material → Normally centrifugally discharged → Concentrate / Tailings hopper two-way collection

Selection AdviceSelection Advice

  1. Match Belt Width First: B500/B650 in the model number indicates belt width (mm). For existing B500 belts, choose the B500 series; for B650 belts, choose the B650 series. For wider belts such as B800 or B1000, custom roller lengths are available.
  2. Select Roller Diameter by Capacity: Low capacity (≤20 t/h) choose Ø320-400mm; medium capacity (30-50 t/h) choose Ø500mm; high capacity (60-80 t/h) choose Ø630mm. Larger roller diameter gives larger magnetic wrap angle, longer separation zone, higher capacity and recovery rate.
  3. Proper Splitter Plate Setting: Install an adjustable splitter plate below the magnetic roller to physically separate concentrate and tailings. By adjusting the plate position, you can flexibly balance concentrate grade and recovery rate — plate close to roller (higher grade), plate away from roller (higher recovery).
  4. Multi-Stage Series Waste Removal: For low-grade lean magnetite, recommend installing one dry magnetic roller after coarse crushing and another after medium crushing. After coarse crushing, remove large waste rock (50-30mm); after medium crushing, remove medium waste rock (30-10mm). Two-stage waste rejection can increase the feed ore grade by 5-10 percentage points.
