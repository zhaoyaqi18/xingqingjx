Product OverviewProduct Overview

The screw conveyor is a tubular enclosed conveying equipment that uses a rotating helical screw blade to transport powder, granular and small-lump materials along a trough or tube. The GX series screw conveyor consists of a screw shaft, casing, drive unit, and inlet/outlet ports. As the screw rotates, material is pushed forward along the tube by the rotating helical flights, similar to a Archimedes screw. The enclosed tubular design prevents dust leakage and material contamination, making it suitable for handling cement, fly ash, chemical powders, grain and other fine materials. The screw conveyor can be arranged horizontally or at an inclination up to 20 degrees, with multiple configurations including single-tube, twin-tube, and jacketed designs for cooling or heating applications. It is widely used in mining, metallurgy, cement, construction materials, chemical, food processing and grain handling industries for short-distance material conveying.

Working PrincipleWorking Principle

1

Feed

The moving jaw retreats, increasing chamber volume, and material feeds into the chamber by gravity.

2

Crushing

The moving jaw advances, compressing and shearing the material between moving and fixed jaw plates, causing fragmentation.

3

Discharge

When crushed material is smaller than the discharge opening, it is automatically discharged from the crushing chamber bottom.

4

Cycle

Each eccentric shaft revolution completes one "feed-crush-discharge" cycle for continuous crushing.

Crushing Force Transmission Path
Motor→V-belt→Belt Pulley→Eccentric Shaft→Moving Jaw→Toggle Plate→Frame

Selection AdviceSelection Advice

  1. Material Hardness：For Mohs hardness 7-8 (granite, basalt), choose PE-600×900 and above models with high manganese steel jaw plates for extended wear life。
  2. Capacity Requirements:For daily capacity under 500 tons, choose PE-400×600 ~ PE-600×900；For thousand-ton production lines, choose PE-900×1200 models and above。
  3. PE vs PEX Selection：PE SeriesSuitable for primary crushing，PEX SeriesSuitable for secondary crushing，Can be combined to form a complete crushing process。
  4. Wet/Sticky Materials：Increase discharge opening or add drying pretreatment to prevent material clogging，Ensure continuous stable production。
