Product Introduction

The mobile crushing station (crawler/wheel type) is a high-efficiency mobile crushing equipment developed by XingQing Machinery for mining, construction waste treatment, and quarry applications. It integrates feeding, crushing, screening and conveying in one unit. It can be flexibly moved to the job site, greatly reducing material transportation costs. Equipped with jaw crusher, impact crusher or cone crusher as the main unit, with processing capacity covering 50-650 TPH, widely applicable for crushing various rocks, ores and construction waste.
