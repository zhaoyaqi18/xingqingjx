Product Introduction

The hydraulic box sand maker combines the advantages of third-generation sand makers, hammer crushers, impact crushers and VSI crushers. It features a super-large arc crushing chamber using the rock-on-rock crushing principle. The crushing chamber has a screening cavity with grate slots. The special blow bar developed by our company achieves a revolutionary breakthrough in hammer use.Achieving low-cost operation. This product is widely applicable to various industries including ore, cement, refractory materials, bauxite clinker, emery, glass raw materials, machine-made building sand, stone and various metallurgical slags.
